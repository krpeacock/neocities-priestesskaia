T4T Leather Community

A trans-for-trans leather community, co-organized by Madam Folly, Priestess Kaia, and a host of other leatherqueers

The community is pretty new - founded in 2025 but with growing numbers. We meet every fourth Tuesday at Mothership from 6 - 9pm. It's worth hopping in the Signal chat or following us on Instagram to get reminders and announcements!
